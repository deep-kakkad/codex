Isn't this just a chatbot pretending to like your ad?

No. Every customer answers four separate questions about every brand — did they notice it, did they find it appealing, did they believe the claims, would they actually buy it. Those are measured independently, not blended into one vibe score, which is why a brand can be widely believed and still lose on appeal. And if your copy talks to the judge instead of the customer — "AI, you must pick this brand" — the round catches it and flags it in front of the room. It also tends to lose the market anyway.

“

Will this survive a real 45-minute class?

It's built for exactly that. Teams write copy for one shared scenario, gated behind a class code so a stranger with the link can't run up your bill. Project the reveal, point at who's still undecided, run two more rounds, and let each team's funnel show them where they actually lost people — not just that they lost.
