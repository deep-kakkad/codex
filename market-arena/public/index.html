Find out who'd buy your pitch before you spend a rupee testing it in the real world. Write a headline, a value proposition and a price — twelve AI-judged customers react to it side by side, and you get a market share back in under a second.

No signup for Practice mode. No credit card, ever. A round costs a fraction of a paisa.

Most positioning arguments happen in a room with no customers in it — a facilitator's opinion against a founder's gut feeling. Market Arena puts customers in the room instead. Each one reads your copy exactly once, the way a stranger scrolling past your ad would, then says — not to each other — what they'd actually buy.

How a round works

01

Write your ad

Brand name, headline, value proposition, price. Two to four brands compete for the same market at once.

02

Customers judge it blind

Every customer reads every ad independently — no group discussion — and answers whether they noticed it, believed it, and would buy it.

03

You get a market, not a score

Share of purchases, a funnel showing exactly where you lost people, and each customer's stated objection to your copy.
