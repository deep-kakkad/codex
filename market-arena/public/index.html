How it works

One connected system, not three separate steps.

→

All contenders are evaluated against the same twelve simulated buyers. The buyers stay still — the propositions move into their arena.

→

Every buyer independently chooses among the contenders in front of them.

Use cases

Four ways teams actually use it.

Positioning

Which promise should lead?

Compare different ways of framing the same product. "Save 5 hours every week" vs. "Never update this spreadsheet again."

Pricing

Does the cheaper offer actually win?

Change price while holding everything else constant, and see who it actually takes share from.

Copy

Which headline earns the choice?

Test specificity, framing, proof, urgency or benefit against the same panel.

Workshops

Turn marketing theory into a live experiment.

Let a room predict the winner. Run the arena. Reveal the decisions. Debate why the market behaved differently from the room.

Under the hood

Decisions, not chatbot opinions.

Market Arena uses TypeSafe's Jev model to evaluate structured choices and return probabilistic decisions. Jev is built for typed probabilistic decisions rather than free-form conversational generation.

That matters because Market Arena is asking which option? — not write me a paragraph about these options.

Trust

A simulator, not a crystal ball.

Use Market Arena for

  • Generating hypotheses
  • Comparing competing messages
  • Finding possible objections
  • Exploring pricing framing
  • Workshops and teaching
  • Deciding what deserves a real-world test

Don't use it as

  • Actual customer research
  • Sales forecasting
  • Proof of product-market fit
  • A substitute for A/B testing
  • A statistically representative survey

Market Arena gives you a cheap directional signal before you pay for an expensive real one. When the decision matters, validate it with actual customers.
