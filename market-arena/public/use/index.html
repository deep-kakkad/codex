What do you want to test?

Headlines, prices, product ads, launches, landing pages and emails: test any of them on simulated buyers before you spend on the real thing.

Test your SaaS headline before you ship itPut two to four headlines in front of simulated buyers: operators, IT buyers, freelancers and team leads. See which one they pick, who walked away, and the exact line that put them off.Test how you say your price"Free, then $3 a month" and "$6.99 a month" land differently on different people. See who picks which, and how many walk away because of the price itself.Test your product ad before you pay for clicksSee which ad buyers would stop scrolling for, which one they believe, and which one they'd actually buy from. Then spend on the one that earned it.Launching something people have never tried?When buyers don't know the category yet, the first message decides everything. Test energy, purity and fun against each other and see who each one wins.Test your landing page copy against a competitor'sPaste your page and a competitor's. Each becomes a version, filled from what the page actually says. Then see who buyers pick, and why.Test your email copy before the sendTwo versions of the same email, one panel of simulated readers. See which one they'd act on, and which line lost the rest.
