Cold brew comes to India

Three brands, three stories: BrewRush sells energy, PureBean sells purity, ChillSip sells fun. Most of these buyers have never had cold brew.

From the ready-made case “Cold brew comes to India”, with buyers from 4 groups: Hustling professionals, Students on a budget, Health-conscious and Filter-coffee loyalists. Open it to run it as it is, or put your own ads in.

How it works

  1. Write two to four versions.Type them, paste a page link, or upload each version's image.
  2. Pick your buyers.Use the case's panel, edit it, or draft buyers from your own reviews and notes.
  3. Run the round.Every buyer reads every version and picks one, or walks away. You watch them decide.

What you find out

  • Which story wins with people who have never tried the category.
  • Where each ad loses people: noticed, interested, believed, bought.
  • How many buyers walk away from all of them, the real size of the challenge.
  • Which group to launch to first.

Questions

Can the buyers know about my market?

Yes. Research the market before round 1 and every buyer reads the strongest findings from research, news and social chatter first. It costs 10 credits and locks in for the project.

Can I use my own customer research?

Yes. Paste reviews, interview notes or survey answers and draft buyers from them. You approve every buyer before they join.

What does it cost?

New accounts get 100 free credits. Rounds are free during the beta; research, image reading and questions cost credits.

The honest part. The buyers are simulated. A round tells you which versions deserve a real test and which to drop, not how many you'll sell.
