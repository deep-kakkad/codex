Your page against theirs

Shown here with the ready-made project-tool case. Put your own pages in with one link each.

From the ready-made case “A project tool enters a crowded market”, with buyers from 4 groups: Startup operators, Enterprise IT buyers, Freelancers & solopreneurs and Team leads at mid-size companies. Open it to run it as it is, or put your own ads in.

How it works

  1. Write two to four versions.Type them, paste a page link, or upload each version's image.
  2. Pick your buyers.Use the case's panel, edit it, or draft buyers from your own reviews and notes.
  3. Run the round.Every buyer reads every version and picks one, or walks away. You watch them decide.

What you find out

  • Whether your page wins the buyers you're after, or theirs does.
  • Which of your lines is doing the work, marked on the page.
  • The group you're losing, and the reason they give.
  • One change to try next, and a round that measures it.

Questions

How does it read a page?

It fetches the page once, pulls out the name, headline, value proposition and price, and marks every field as the page's exact words or condensed. It costs 1 credit and the page isn't stored.

Can I compare more than two pages?

Up to four versions per round.

Can I share the result?

Yes. Every round has a share link that opens the report read-only.

The honest part. The buyers are simulated. A round tells you which versions deserve a real test and which to drop, not how many you'll sell.
