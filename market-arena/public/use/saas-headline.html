How it works

  1. Write two to four versions.Type them, paste a page link, or upload each version's image.
  2. Pick your buyers.Use the case's panel, edit it, or draft buyers from your own reviews and notes.
  3. Run the round.Every buyer reads every version and picks one, or walks away. You watch them decide.

What you find out

  • Which headline each group of buyers picks, and by how much.
  • The line in each ad that did the work, and the line that cost it.
  • Why the rest said no: price, doubt, the wrong fit, or a confusing offer.
  • What to change for round 2, so the next test tells you something new.

Questions

Can I test my real homepage copy?

Yes. Paste a page link and the version fills itself from what the page says, or type the copy in. Test it against a rewrite, or against a competitor's page.

Who are the buyers?

Simulated people with written profiles, in groups you can edit. The ready-made case has operators, IT buyers, freelancers and team leads. You can add your own, or draft them from your reviews and interview notes.

Does it replace an A/B test?

No. It tells you which versions deserve a real test and which to drop before you spend on traffic.

The honest part. The buyers are simulated. A round tells you which versions deserve a real test and which to drop, not how many you'll sell.
