How it works

  1. Write two to four versions.Type them, paste a page link, or upload each version's image.
  2. Pick your buyers.Use the case's panel, edit it, or draft buyers from your own reviews and notes.
  3. Run the round.Every buyer reads every version and picks one, or walks away. You watch them decide.

What you find out

  • How often "the price feels too high" is the reason people pass, for each version.
  • Which groups the price scares off, and which it doesn't.
  • Whether a cheaper-looking price wins, or a clearer one.
  • How many buyers walk away from every option.

Questions

Can I test only the price line?

Yes. Keep everything else identical and change just the price. Then the difference in the result is what the price was worth.

Can I add an offer or a free trial?

Yes. Switch on the offer field before round 1 and every version gets one, so the comparison stays fair.

Is this real willingness to pay?

No. The buyers are simulated. Use it to narrow down which price framings to test for real.

The honest part. The buyers are simulated. A round tells you which versions deserve a real test and which to drop, not how many you'll sell.
