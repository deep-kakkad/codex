Important disclaimer

Useful fake customers.
Not real customers.

Market Arena is a simulator. Use it to spot weak ideas, find objections, compare versions, and decide what deserves a real test. Don't use it to claim "50% of the market will buy this." They won't — these are simulated buyers.

Good for
  • Comparing messages
  • Exploring positioning
  • Pressure-testing pricing
  • Finding objections
  • Generating hypotheses
  • Teaching marketing decisions
Not a replacement for
  • Customer interviews
  • Market research
  • A/B testing
  • Sales forecasting
  • Statistically representative surveys

When the decision really matters, test it with actual humans.

How it's built

Wondering what's making the choices?

Market Arena uses TypeSafe's Jev model to simulate structured decisions. Instead of asking an AI "which headline is better?", we give each simulated buyer the same options and ask them to choose. Then Market Arena combines those choices into the result you see. That's it.
