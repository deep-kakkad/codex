A simulator, not a crystal ball

Direction before validation.

Market Arena is designed to pressure-test ideas, generate hypotheses and expose possible objections before you run expensive real-world experiments.

Good for
  • Comparing messages
  • Exploring positioning
  • Pressure-testing pricing
  • Finding objections
  • Generating hypotheses
  • Teaching marketing decisions
Not a replacement for
  • Customer interviews
  • Market research
  • A/B testing
  • Sales forecasting
  • Statistically representative surveys

Use the arena to decide what deserves a real test.
