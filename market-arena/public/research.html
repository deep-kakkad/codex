Research your market

Market Arena searches the public web — research papers, established news, industry publications and blogs, and real social and forum discussion — and summarises what buyers in this market care about and what holds them back. Every finding links to the page it came from.

Usually takes 15–30 seconds. Results are fetched fresh each time and are not saved — only the short findings you choose to give your buyers are kept, with your project.

Searching…
  1. Searching research, news, industry writing and social discussion
  2. Reading what the sources say about buyers
  3. Checking every finding against a page that was actually opened
  4. Screening the findings before any buyer sees them
Findings come from public sources found by live web search. They describe the market in general, not your customers, and can be wrong or out of date — follow the links before relying on one.
