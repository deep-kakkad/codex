Questions

What if something fails halfway?

You get your credits back. We take them when you ask and return them automatically if the answer doesn't arrive.

Why are rounds free?

Because a round costs us a fraction of a cent. They're free during the beta, up to 20 an hour from one connection, so nobody can run up a bill on a public link.

Do I need a card to start?

No. Every new account gets 100 credits. That's ten questions or research runs, or a hundred pages read.

How do I buy more?

By card, through Stripe, once top-ups open. Credits land in your account the moment Stripe confirms the payment.

Buyers are simulated by TypeSafe's Jev model. Use the results to pick what deserves a real test. They are not market research.
