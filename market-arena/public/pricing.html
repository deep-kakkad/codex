Pricing

Start with 100 free credits, no card needed. You only spend credits on the things that cost money to run: questions and market research. Rounds are free while Market Arena is in beta.

Questions

What happens if a question or research run fails?

Nothing is charged. Credits are taken when you ask, and given straight back if the answer doesn't arrive.

Why are rounds free?

A round costs a fraction of a cent to run. They are free during the beta, up to 20 an hour from one connection, so a public link can't be used to run up a bill.

Do I need a card to start?

No. Every new account gets 100 credits, which is ten questions or research runs.

How do I pay for more?

By card through Stripe, once top-ups open. Credits appear in your account as soon as Stripe confirms the payment.

Buyers are simulated by TypeSafe's Jev model. Treat the results as a way to decide what deserves a real test, not as market research.
