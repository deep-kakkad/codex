# How Market Arena talks

Two teachers. David Ogilvy: the reader is not a moron, facts sell better than
adjectives, and the headline has to do most of the work. Neville Medhora: write
the way you'd explain it to a friend at a bar, short lines, plain words, and a
bit of fun when it earns its place.

## The rules

1. **Say the benefit, then prove it.** "Find the ad that wins before you pay for
   it" beats "Competitive message testing". Follow a promise with a number, a
   mechanism or an example, never another promise.
2. **Specific beats clever.** "12 buyers, 3 ads, 40 seconds" beats "fast,
   powerful insights". Use the real numbers we have.
3. **Short.** One idea per sentence. Most sentences under 15 words. If a
   paragraph runs past three lines, it's probably two paragraphs or a list.
4. **Plain words.** "Picked" not "selected". "Walked away" not "abstained".
   "Why they said no" not "objection analysis". Explain a stat the first time in
   words a busy founder gets on one read.
5. **Talk to one person.** "You", "your ads", "your buyers". Not "users", not
   "teams leverage".
6. **One smile per screen, max.** A light line where the reader is relaxed
   (landing page, empty states, loading). Never in an error, never near money,
   never at the expense of the reader.
7. **Buttons say what happens.** "Run round 2", "Read this page", "Draft 8
   buyers". Not "Submit", not "Continue".
8. **Errors say what happened and what to do.** And, when money is involved,
   whether they were charged.

## The honesty rules (these never bend)

- The buyers are **simulated**. Say so plainly and early. Results decide what
  deserves a real test; they are not market research.
- Buyers answer **typed questions**. We never write a line in a buyer's voice
  or put words in their mouth, so no fake quotes, no "Priya says…".
- No invented numbers, customers, logos or testimonials. Every number on the
  landing page comes from our own tests or the replayed round.
- Ties are ties. Under five points is "too close to call", not a win.

## House words

| Say | Not |
| --- | --- |
| version (the thing you test) | pitch, contender, variant |
| ad (what a buyer sees) | creative, asset |
| buyers, the panel | personas, respondents, users |
| round | test run, simulation run |
| picked, chose | selected, converted |
| walked away / bought nothing | abstained, no-purchase |
| why they said no | objection analysis |
| average chance of being picked ("41% chance") | average choice probability |
| picked outright (their top pick) | top-choice count |

No em dashes in new copy. A comma, a full stop or a colon does the job.
